# Deck Discovery Brief

Adapt this structure only when a durable brief helps a complex communication
job. Reuse the existing meaning source when one exists; omit irrelevant fields.
This is a working document, not audience-facing slide copy.

## Communication decision

- Audience, including anyone likely to receive a forwarded copy:
- Desired decision, action, understanding or belief change:
- Main skepticism, what the audience already knows and which of the material
  they wrote or decided:
- Register, explanatory or peer, and why:
- Live, async, leave-behind or mixed use:
- Chosen medium, artifact owner and reason:
- Density and length constraints:
- Language, brand, accessibility, editability and delivery needs:

## Argument

- Core thesis:
- Audience's starting and desired understanding:
- Evidence sequence:
- Material alternative, trade-off or objection:
- Ask or next step:

- Canonical source artifact or outline:

- Agent-settled content open to revision within the mandate:

- Source facts still needing verification:

## Backstage guardrails

- Unsupported or forbidden claims:
- Corrections, framing decisions, reasons for a cut and evidence classes:
- Evidence limitations and confidential material:
- Open questions that change the result:
- Assumptions used to proceed:
- Required review checkpoints or publication authority:

## Artifact handoff, when needed

Pass the existing source, audience, register, outcome, mode, evidence,
constraints and locks to the selected artifact owner. Record where the
resulting artifact and its applicable rendering/inspection evidence live,
without claiming work that owner has not done.
