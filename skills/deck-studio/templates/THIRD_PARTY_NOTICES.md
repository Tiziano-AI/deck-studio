# Template provenance and licenses

The 34 `bold` systems and 12 `safe-presets` systems adapt Zara Zhang's
[Bold Template Pack](https://github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides/blob/9906a34d640d2111f724544cbc50f7f130569ae1/bold-template-pack/README.md)
and [Style Presets](https://github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides/blob/9906a34d640d2111f724544cbc50f7f130569ae1/STYLE_PRESETS.md)
from `frontend-slides` commit `9906a34d640d2111f724544cbc50f7f130569ae1`.
The original MIT notice is reproduced below. The Bold Template Pack names
`beautiful-html-templates` as its ancestor; the pinned commit belongs to
`frontend-slides`, not that ancestor repository.

The ten `house` systems are original Deck Studio work under the root MIT
license. Font names in recipes are not font files or blanket licenses to
redistribute those fonts; keep each chosen font's own terms when localizing it.
The bundled SmoothOperator brand compositions retain their own brand identity
and are for authorized brand work. Their Raleway and Victor Mono font files are
licensed separately under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full family notices
are beside those files in `house/_smoothoperator-assets/fonts/`; copy the matching
notice whenever a font family is copied into a delivered deck. The gateway's
font copies carry the same notices in `publisher/static-server/ui/fonts/`.
