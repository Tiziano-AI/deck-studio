# Product Cinema starter composition prompts

These are generic starting structures, not implemented specialist layouts. Only the cover skeleton is supplied in `preview.html`; author and validate every production composition against its content.

- Cover: one locked title or thesis with supplied context; retain needed subtitle and qualifications.
- Statement: a dominant claim with a supporting phrase when one exists. Visual hierarchy cannot strengthen the claim.
- Two-column: related blocks aligned on a clear axis; preserve reading order and qualifications. Unequal content can need unequal widths.
- Claim-evidence: pair a claim with its actual chart, quotation, image, or proof and a readable source. An empty proof slot is not permission to invent evidence.

Specialist tables, code listings, diagrams, ledgers, or cinematic sequences require new layout work and rendered checks; this starter makes no readiness claim for them. Use 1920x1080 stage coordinates, preserve readable type, and split content without semantic loss when fit requires it.
