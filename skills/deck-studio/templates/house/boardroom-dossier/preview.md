# Boardroom Dossier starter preview guidance

Visual direction: navy dossier pages, strict rules, red/amber/green decision marks, serif headlines with mono evidence labels.

`preview.html` is a single-cover skeleton on the canonical fixed-stage runtime. Its illustrative communication copy is shared across the eight starters; it is not customer evidence or a claim that specialized layouts exist. Proposed fonts are not bundled, so system-role fallbacks apply until real assets are loaded.

For a real style comparison, use identical representative locked content across candidates. Include the hard content case when a cover cannot test fit; this recipe imposes no fixed preview count or human approval gate. Keep selection labels outside the slide and preserve meaning, qualifications, evidence, sequence, and ask.
